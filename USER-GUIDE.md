# Water Station User Guide

This guide explains the everyday steps for selling products, taking orders, and delivering them. It follows the pages and buttons in the app and is written for customers, staff, and riders.

## 1. Add products to the shop

Staff can create and maintain products from **Inventory**.

1. Open **Inventory** and select **Add product**.
2. Enter the product name and, if useful, a short description.
3. Choose the container type. The shop sells water by the gallon.
4. Enter the price and, optionally, add a product photo.
5. Enter **Stock on hand** and a **Reorder level**:
   - Stock on hand is how many gallons are currently available.
   - The reorder level is the point at which inventory warns staff that stock is low.
6. Leave **Active (visible in the shop)** checked if customers should be able to order it. Uncheck it to hide the product from the shop.
7. Select **Save product**.

The product now appears in the inventory list. Active products with available stock can appear in the customer shop. Products with no stock cannot be added to a cart.

### Update products or receive more stock

- From **Inventory**, select **Edit** beside a product to change its name, description, container type, price, photo, stock count, reorder level, or shop visibility. Select **Save changes** when finished.
- To record a delivery from a supplier, open the product's **History**. Enter the quantity received and, if known, the supplier, unit cost, and a note. Select **Record restock**.
- Use **Adjust stock** on the edit page when stock needs to be corrected, marked damaged, or adjusted for another reason. The product's history shows recorded stock changes and the resulting balance.

## 2. Customer places an order

### Choose products

1. Open **Shop** to browse the products, prices, and stock availability.
2. For each product, choose a quantity and select **Add to cart**.
3. Open **Cart** to review the order:
   - Change a quantity to update it.
   - Use the remove button to take a product out.
   - Select **Keep shopping** to add more products.
4. When the cart is correct, select **Proceed to checkout**.

If a product is out of stock or there is not enough stock for the requested quantity, reduce the quantity or choose another available product.

### Complete checkout

On the checkout page:

1. Choose **Delivery** or **Pickup at station**.
   - For delivery, select a saved delivery address. The delivery fee depends on the area selected for that address.
   - If there is no saved address, open **Addresses** first and add one. Enter the address and delivery area. A map pin is optional, but helps the rider find the location.
   - Pickup does not need a delivery address or delivery fee.
2. Check the contact phone number and choose the delivery or pickup date and time slot.
3. Add any useful optional note, such as a landmark, gate code, or who the rider should ask for.
4. Review the item subtotal and delivery fee shown in the order summary.
5. Choose **Cash on delivery or pickup** or **GCash**.
   - With cash, pay when the order is handed over.
   - With GCash, continue to the on-screen payment step and complete payment. The order cannot move into preparation until GCash payment is complete.
6. Select **Place order** or continue to GCash, depending on the payment choice.

After placing the order, the customer can open **My orders**, select the order, and check its status, schedule, items, payment, and delivery information. An order can be cancelled by the customer while it is still **Pending**.

## 3. Staff confirms and prepares the order

Staff handle new orders from **Order board**. The board groups active orders by status. Select an order card to open its details.

1. Review the order number, customer name and phone, requested date and time, delivery address or pickup method, items, total, payment status, and any customer note.
2. For a new order, select **Mark as Confirmed** to accept it.
3. When work begins, select **Mark as Preparing**.
   - Starting preparation reduces the available product stock.
   - If stock is not available, do not continue preparation; contact the customer or use the order's available cancellation or rejection option as appropriate.
   - A GCash order must show as paid before it can be moved into preparation.
4. Follow the order's status on the board and open **Order history** to find completed or cancelled orders.

The order detail page also shows a history of status changes. If staff cancel or reject an order, the app asks for a reason, which the customer can see.

## 4. Assign a delivery

For a delivery order, open its details from **Order board**. Once the order is **Preparing**, use the **Assign rider** section:

1. Select a rider from the list. A rider marked **busy** may already have another delivery.
2. Select **Assign rider**.
3. The order moves to **Rider assigned**, and the assigned rider can find it in **My deliveries**.

Assign the rider after moving the order into **Preparing**. The assignment option may be visible while an order is **Confirmed**, but the usual sequence is to start preparation first.

## 5. Rider delivers the order

1. Open **My deliveries** and select the assigned order.
2. Check the customer name and phone, delivery address, scheduled time, ordered items, and customer notes.
3. Use **Get directions** to open directions. If the customer added a map pin, the map can show the pinned destination.
4. Check the payment reminder:
   - For cash orders, collect the displayed total from the customer at handoff.
   - For GCash orders that are already paid, there is nothing to collect.
5. Select **Start delivery** when leaving with the order. Its status changes to **Out for delivery**.
6. While the order is out for delivery, the rider can select **Turn on** under **Share my location** and allow location access. This lets the customer see the rider's location on the order page. Sharing location is optional.
7. Hand the order to the customer, collect cash if required, then select **Mark as delivered**.

After delivery, staff can open the order and select **Mark as Completed** to close it. The customer can see delivery progress in **My orders**. A receipt is available from the order page once payment is complete or the order has been delivered.

## 6. Pickup orders

Pickup orders use the same customer steps, except the customer selects **Pickup at station** instead of delivery. No rider is assigned. Staff move the order from **Pending** to **Confirmed**, then **Preparing**, then **Ready for pickup**. When the customer collects it, staff can mark it **Completed**.

## Order status quick reference

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | The order has been placed and is waiting for staff review. |
| **Confirmed** | Staff accepted the order. |
| **Preparing** | Staff are getting the products ready. |
| **Ready for pickup** | A pickup order is ready at the station. |
| **Rider assigned** | A rider has been selected for a delivery order. |
| **Out for delivery** | The rider has started the trip to the customer. |
| **Delivered** | The rider has recorded the handoff. |
| **Completed** | The order is closed. |
| **Cancelled / Rejected** | The order will not continue; staff provide a reason when cancelling or rejecting it. |
